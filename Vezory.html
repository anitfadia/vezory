About us

Built by engineers, for what the markets are becoming.

Vezory is an early-stage quantitative trading firm, founded on the belief that markets reward the prepared, not the loud. We are at the very beginning of our journey — small, deliberate, and building in the open.

Our work today is foundational: research, discipline, and the long study of market structure that every serious trading firm is built on. We are not pretending to be further along than we are. We are here for the long arc, not the headline.

What we do

Three disciplines, one conviction.

Vezory is organized around the three pillars that will define our firm as we grow — research, infrastructure, and execution.

01

Quantitative research

Studying market microstructure, price behavior, and the statistical signals that separate noise from opportunity.

02

Algorithmic infrastructure

Building the software that turns a strategy into disciplined, repeatable execution — reliable before it needs to be fast.

03

Disciplined execution

Treating risk as a first-class concern, not an afterthought, from the very first line of strategy we write.
